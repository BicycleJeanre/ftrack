# Glossary

## 1.0 Glossary

A reference guide to common terms used throughout FTrack.

## 1.1 A

- **Account**: A place where money lives or is owed. Examples: checking account, savings account, credit card, loan. Accounts are fundamental to FTrack data model.
- **Account Type**: The classification of an account. Types include Asset, Liability, Income, and Expense. These help organize and categorize your financial information.
- **Actual**: A dated occurrence that records what happened in the real world.
  It keeps its linked plan and frozen baseline available for comparison.

## 1.2 B

- **Balance**: The amount of money in an account at a specific point in time. Opening balance is your starting point; projected balance shows where you will be in the future.
- **Budget**: A workflow preset that opens Plan & Actuals in Period view for
  baseline, current-plan, actual, commitment, and variance tracking.

## 1.3 C

- **Compound Interest**: A periodic change that applies exponential growth, like investment returns. Interest earned also earns interest, creating accelerating growth.

## 1.4 D

- **Dual-Entry Money Movement**: A movement involving two accounts, with a
  source and destination that preserve the accounting direction.

## 1.5 E

- **Expense**: Money that flows out, such as rent, utilities, groceries, or subscriptions. Categorized as an Account Type for organizational purposes.

## 1.6 F

- **Flat Change**: A periodic change that adds or subtracts a fixed dollar amount each period. Example: salary increases by 100 dollars quarterly.
- **Forecast**: See Projection. The process of predicting future balances from
  current accounts, unresolved plan occurrences, and actuals.

## 1.7 I

- **Income**: Money flowing in, such as salary, bonuses, or interest earned. Categorized as an Account Type for organization.

## 1.8 L

- **Liability**: Money you owe to others. Examples: credit cards, loans, mortgages. Tracked as an Account Type.

## 1.9 O

- **Opening Balance**: The starting balance of an account, as of the current date. This is the basis for all future projections.

## 1.10 P

- **Percentage Change**: A periodic change that applies a percentage adjustment each period. Example: salary grows 3 percent annually.
- **Periodic Change**: An automatic adjustment to a plan-rule amount at regular
  intervals. Used to model salary raises, inflation, and other gradual changes.
- **Plan Rule**: The reusable definition of an expected money movement. It can
  be one-time or recurring and can include periodic changes.
- **Plan Occurrence**: One dated instance resolved from a plan rule, including
  any period-specific adjustment, skip, actual, or frozen baseline.
- **Projection**: A forecast of future account balances based on current
  balances and the resolved Plan & Actuals timeline.

## 1.11 Q

- **Quarterly**: Occurring every 3 months. A recurrence pattern for plan rules
  that repeat 4 times per year.

## 1.12 R

- **Recurrence**: A repeating schedule for a plan rule. Patterns include weekly,
  monthly, quarterly, and yearly.

## 1.13 S

- **Scenario**: A complete financial plan or what-if situation. It contains its
  own accounts, plan rules, occurrences, baselines, actuals, and projections.

## 1.14 T

- **Transaction**: The stored domain record used for a one-time or recurring
  plan rule. In the interface, rules and their dated results are managed
  together through Plan & Actuals.

## 1.15 V

- **Variance**: The difference between the frozen baseline, current plan,
  forecast contribution, or actual amount.

## 1.16 W

- **What-If Scenario**: A scenario designed to explore a specific change or decision. Example: what if I get a raise or what if I buy a car.
